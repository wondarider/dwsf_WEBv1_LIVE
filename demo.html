About DWSF

The Digital Wellbeing Security Framework (DWSF) is a practical maturity assessment that helps organisations understand and strengthen digital wellbeing, drawing on proven cybersecurity approaches to identify gaps and build resilience.


This demo is a simplified snapshot covering just Education and Governance. The full framework goes further — covering students, educators, and parents — and produces a benchmark score, an uplift roadmap, and an executive report.

Context

The ability to identify and safely respond to common online risks, including harmful content, suspicious contact, cyberbullying, scams, manipulative platform features, and AI-enabled harms such as fake content and impersonation.

1. Education

Why this matters

The digital world presents opportunities as well as risks. The ability to recognise potential harms, make informed choices, and seek help when needed is a key part of building lifelong digital resilience.

Context

Digital Wellbeing governance ensures structures, processes, and leadership practices enable digital wellbeing initiatives to be planned, implemented, measured, and continuously improved.

2. Governance

Why this matters

Strong governance ensures Digital Wellbeing is not left to chance. It provides the leadership, accountability, and structure needed to identify risks early, prioritise action, and sustain positive outcomes over time.
