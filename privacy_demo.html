Privacy Policy

Digital Wellbeing Security Framework (DWSF)

1. Who we are

The Digital Wellbeing Security Framework (DWSF) is a self-funded project with no affiliation with any third party. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through our website and demonstration benchmarking tools.

2. What we collect

We only collect personal information that you choose to give us. Currently, this is limited to:

  • Name and email address — collected only if you use the "Share my results" option after completing a benchmarking demo, so that our team can review your results and follow up with you.
  • Your quiz responses — when you choose to share your results, the answers you selected for each question, along with your resulting score and domain breakdown, are included in that submission so our team has the full context when reviewing it.

If you complete the benchmarking demo but do not use the "Share my results" option, none of this information leaves your browser — your score and answers are calculated and displayed locally, and nothing is transmitted to us. Note that under our current plan, sharing your results does not automatically send you a copy back by email — if you'd like a copy for your own records, you're welcome to take a screenshot or contact us directly.

3. Why we collect it

We collect your name and email so our team can review the results you've shared and, if appropriate, follow up with you. Your quiz responses and score are included in that same submission purely so we have the full picture — they are not used by us for any other purpose, such as analysis, profiling, or marketing, unless you separately and explicitly agree to that elsewhere.

4. How it's handled

Report requests are processed using Web3Forms, a third-party form-delivery service, which transmits your submission — including your name, email, quiz responses, and score — to our email inbox. We don't operate our own database to store submissions beyond what's needed to send the report. Web3Forms' handling of your data is governed by their own privacy policy, available on their website.

5. Storage and retention

We retain the email received via Web3Forms only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil your request and respond to any related follow-up, after which it is deleted. We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, loss, and unauthorised access.

6. Access, correction, and deletion

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete any personal information we hold about you at any time by contacting us using the details below.

7. Cookies and analytics

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may log IP addresses as part of standard web request handling. This site uses Plausible Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. Plausible is a privacy-friendly analytics tool that does not use cookies and does not collect any personal information. It only collects aggregated, anonymized data such as page views, referring websites, and general location (country/region level). No personal data is collected, stored, or shared. You can read more about Plausible's approach at Plausible Analytics.

8. Complaints

If you have a concern about how we've handled your personal information, please contact us first so we can try to resolve it. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, particularly as privacy law continues to change through 2026 and beyond. The date below reflects the last update.

10. Contact us

For any privacy questions or requests, contact: contact@digitalwellbeingsecurityframework.com
