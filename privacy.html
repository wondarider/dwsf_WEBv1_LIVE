Privacy Policy

Last updated: 04 November 2026

Overview

The Digital Wellbeing Security Framework (DWSF) is a self-funded benchmarking tool that helps schools assess and improve their digital safety maturity. This policy explains how DWSF handles information in connection with this website and the assessment framework.

What information DWSF does not collect or store

DWSF does not collect, store, process, or have access to:

  • Student personal information or records
  • Staff or parent personal information
  • Assessment responses, scores, or results
  • Any sensitive or identifiable data relating to a school community

All assessment activity — including responses, scoring, and any resulting reports — takes place entirely within the school's own infrastructure, systems, or nominated tools. DWSF provides the framework, methodology, and guidance materials only. Schools remain fully responsible for how they administer the assessment, and for the storage, security, and handling of any data it generates.

We do not use cookies for advertising or tracking purposes, and do not sell or share any information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data security

Because assessment data never passes through or is stored on DWSF-operated systems, the security and integrity of that data is governed by each school's own IT policies and infrastructure — not by DWSF.

For the limited contact information DWSF does receive (e.g. enquiry emails), reasonable steps are taken to protect it (for example, access is limited to the site owner and emails are stored in a secured inbox). As a self-funded personal project, no enterprise-grade security certification is claimed. Enquiry correspondence is retained only as long as needed to respond, and you may request deletion at any time by contacting us (see below).

Analytics, and Website Delivery and Security

Our website is served through Cloudflare, a content delivery and security service. Because traffic to our site passes through Cloudflare’s network, Cloudflare processes technical information about each visit, including your IP address, browser type, device information, the pages you request, and the date and time of your request. Cloudflare uses this information to deliver the site, protect it against malicious traffic and attacks, and provide us with aggregated traffic statistics, such as the number of requests, the countries visitors come from, and the types of devices used. We do not add any analytics scripts or tracking cookies of our own to your browser for this purpose, although Cloudflare may set strictly necessary cookies for security, for example to distinguish genuine visitors from automated bots. We use this information only to measure traffic, maintain the performance and security of the website, and improve our content. You can read more about how Cloudflare processes this data in accordance with its own privacy policy, available at Cloudflare privacy policy.

Visitors from the UK and European Economic Area

If you are in the UK or the European Economic Area, we process this data on the basis of our legitimate interests in keeping our website secure, reliable, and understanding how it is used (Article 6(1)(f) of the UK GDPR and EU GDPR). Cloudflare acts as our processor, and in some cases as an independent controller for its own security purposes. Cloudflare may process data in countries outside the UK and EEA, including the United States, and relies on appropriate safeguards for these transfers, such as standard contractual clauses or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum. You have the right to access, correct, or erase your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk). For more information on how Cloudflare handles data, see its privacy policy at Cloudflare privacy policy.

Third-party links

This website links to external resources (e.g. eSafety Commissioner, Cyberbullying Research Center). DWSF is not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party sites.

Children's privacy

DWSF's website and framework are intended for use by school staff and administrators, not directly by children. DWSF does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent revision.

Contact

For questions about this policy, or to request deletion of any information you've provided, contact: contact@digitalwellbeingsecurityframework.com
